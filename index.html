Meet The Creator

Aesthetic Living in Modest Square Footage

"You do not need to own a home to love the space you live in."

Hi! I'm Muhammd Umar Afzal, the voice and small-space enthusiast behind HomeDecorFinds. After moving through five different rental apartments in five years, I was tired of living with bland beige builder-grade walls and cluttered surfaces.

I created this platform to prove that renting shouldn't hold you back from high-end interior aesthetics. Through thoughtful palette balance (warm cream, soothing sage, and earthy terracotta), multi-functional storage, and easy DIY woodwork, you can turn any rental box into a welcoming, personalized sanctuary.
